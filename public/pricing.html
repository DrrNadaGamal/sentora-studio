FAQ

Straight answers

Do you write every line of code by hand?

No. I direct each build and use AI coding tools to move fast. I review the result, test it in a real browser and fix problems before I hand it over. You get working software and the source files.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay per project. There are no monthly fees from me. Hosting and domains are billed by their own providers.

What can you build?

WordPress business sites and custom web apps such as dashboards, booking systems, portals and small SaaS tools. I work in English and Arabic, including right-to-left layouts.

Do I need to write a long brief?

No. One sentence is enough to start. If I need more, I ask a few short questions before I quote.

Yes. The Work page has live demos you can open. They use made-up data. I am also finishing a medical clinic website, which is not public yet.
